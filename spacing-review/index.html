The same tokens power utilities and component props: Section gap="24", ButtonWrapper marginTop="32".

Geometry and controls

At 1440: buttons are 48px high with an 8px radius; fields are 64px high with a 16px radius. On mobile, fields start at 56px with an 8px radius and 16px side padding. Borders are 1px. Keyboard focus is 2px with a 2px offset. Icon buttons stay circular and at least 44px.
