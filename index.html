Eco
projects

ECOmbare is Cyprus based environmental agency for people and companies who want to create real environmental impact

Our Signature Programs — for
companies and individuals. Each one
designed to get people outside, create
measurable impact.

1.

Restore forest

We take teams into fire-affected areas to plant trees — and actually learn why it matters. Experts on the ground, hands in the soil, ecosystem restored. We work with Groasis Waterboxx technology — a Dutch innovation that helps trees survive and thrive even in the driest conditions, making every planting count long after the event.

2.

Gift a tree

A tree planted in the name of someone who matters — an employee, a loved one, a milestone. Each tree comes with a personal certificate and a real location. Companies use it as a meaningful gift for birthdays, work anniversaries, or onboarding.

3.

Clean Up Cyprus

Beach and nature cleanups — but not just picking up trash. We run workshops directly on location, using collected waste as the material. Teams learn about pollution, plastic cycles, and what actually happens to what we throw away. Hands-on, eye-opening, and genuinely fun.

4.

Adopt the Hive

Your company places branded hives on the hills of Cyprus — in your colors, on fire-affected land. Teams visit, paint hives, and learn beekeeping hands-on from the local beekeeping community at Oros Maxaira. In return: up to 20kg of honey per hive, bottled with your logo. Seasonal updates and hive visits included.

5.

Revive the Village

A full-day event where a team takes on a Cypriot village — renovating infrastructure, planting trees, restoring heritage sites. Big effort, visible result, strong team bond. The kind of day people talk about for years.

6.

Workshops

Three interactive workshops — Climate Fresk, Biodiversity Collage, Circular Economy Collage — where teams work together to understand climate systems and find real solutions. Plus the EnROADS Simulator, a climate game developed with MIT, where your team makes real policy decisions and sees the impact in real time.

Pilot launch of Groasis Waterboxx technology in Cyprus — restoring a mountain slope damaged by the 2021 wildfire, as part of the Back To The Roots festival. Delivered in partnership with the Cyprus Forestry Department and the Municipality of Arakapas

100

plants with Waterboxx

50

What we did:

Planted 100 endemic species using Groasis Waterboxx technology and 50 without — a side-by-side comparison to generate real survival data for Cypriot conditions. Trained volunteers in long-term monitoring

Why it matters:

This pilot didn't just plant trees — it proved the technology works on Cypriot soil. That data now shapes how ECOmbare trains event managers and selects species for every planting since, including the Souni project with Playrix

Grow Love personalized tree gifting program

“Grow Love” — a personalized tree gifting program for both companies and individuals, planted using Groasis Waterboxx technology across three Cypriot villages

“Grow Love” — a personalized tree gifting program for both companies and individuals, planted using Groasis Waterboxx technology across three Cypriot villages

300+

100%

What we did:

Launched personalized tree certificates — B2B and B2C — where each tree is planted in someone's name and dedicated to a person or moment that matters. Introduced a shared responsibility model: people don't just plant and walk away, they return to care for their tree in the years that follow, while we send updates and growth reports. Why it matters:

This is the proof that environmental impact and personal meaning aren't separate things. People come back. Trees survive. And every certificate carries a story — sometimes two.

Playrix reforestation project

May 2026

First Groasis Waterboxx pilot reforestation on fire-affected land in Cyprus — launched 10 months after the July 2025 wildfire that burned 125 km² and destroyed 43 homes

First Groasis Waterboxx pilot reforestation on fire-affected land in Cyprus — launched 10 months after the July 2025 wildfire that burned 125 km² and destroyed 43 homes

10

What we did:

320 Waterboxx units installed across a fire-damaged hillside. Each unit: 1 endemic tree + 1 shrub. Soil treated with zeolite and mycorrhizal inoculation to restore what the fire destroyed underground. Every plant registered with GPS + QR code for long-term monitoring

Why it matters:

This is a live research project — not a one-day event. The survival data generated here will shape island-wide reforestation strategy for years to come
